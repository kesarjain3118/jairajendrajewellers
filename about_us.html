About Us

Jai Rajendra Jewellers has been a trusted name in fine jewellery for over 20 years in Peddapuram. Founded by the late Sukhraj Indramailji, the showroom is now proudly managed by his sons, Vikas Kumar and Sanjeev Kumar. We are known for designing jewellery that blends traditional craftsmanship with modern elegance.

Every piece is crafted with care, precision, and a strong commitment to quality. Whether it is a simple gold ornament or a detailed diamond piece, our goal is to make your special moments truly memorable.

Our Story

What began as a small family-run jewellery shop has grown into one of the most respected showrooms in the region. Over the years, we have built strong relationships with our customers by offering genuine products, honest service, and a warm shopping experience.

For us, jewellery is not just an accessory—it is a part of your celebrations and memories. That is why we ensure every customer leaves with confidence, satisfaction, and a piece that brings joy.
